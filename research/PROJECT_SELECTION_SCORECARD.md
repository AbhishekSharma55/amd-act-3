# Project selection scorecard

Use after the ACT III strategy research. No project has been selected.

Participant constraint: **$100 AMD allocation supplied by the hackathon, plus $20 personal spending**. Two members. Team size, assumed effort, and artificial build-time limits are not elimination criteria.

## Required gates

Each candidate must have a credible path through these requirements:

- Fit an ACT III challenge, with clarification if it is principally infrastructure or developer tooling.
- Run a meaningful portion of its demonstrated workload on approved AMD infrastructure.
- Produce the required working product, public code, descriptions, video, slides, and application access.
- Fit the monetary budget and available service capabilities, including judging access.
- Use data that is available for the actual demonstration and evaluation.
- Meet the chosen partner's specific eligibility and data conditions, when competing for a partner award.

A requirement with uncertain implementation is a research question. An essential unavailable resource or a rule conflict is a substantive reason to reject or redesign the candidate.

## Compare the official dimensions

The four dimensions below are official criterion names. The questions and scoring anchors are our internal preparation method. No official weights have been confirmed.

| Dimension | Questions | Evidence to seek |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Application of Technology | Does the system work? Is the AMD contribution substantive? Are model and orchestration choices justified? | Actual execution, code, architecture, evaluation, recovery behavior |
| Business Value | Does a specific user need this? Who pays? Is the benefit measured or credibly modeled? | User/process evidence, baseline, operating economics, adoption path |
| Originality | What is the valuable new capability? How does it compare with existing alternatives and current entries? | Competitor comparison and a demonstration of the distinctive behavior |
| Presentation | Can judges quickly understand and inspect the result and proof? | Opening statement, memorable interaction, clear visual evidence |

Internal anchors: **1** = mostly assertion; **2** = plausible but weakly supported; **3** = working and partially evidenced; **4** = strong supporting evidence; **5** = exceptional and distinctive evidence.

Avoid selecting solely by a numerical sum. Inspect the weakest dimension, evidence credibility, financial viability, and whether the distinctive capability survives scrutiny. Scores represent our assessment, not predicted judge votes.

## Questions that change the decision

Prioritize confirming domain data, a real user's task, a competent baseline, the required model capabilities, access to actual AMD execution, and the ability to keep the demo available within budget. A partner prize is a secondary consideration when it improves the same product or offers meaningful additional eligibility.

Ambition remains open. Favor the candidate whose valuable new behavior can be demonstrated and supported most convincingly.
