2

Who can use an event workspace
To create or join an event workspace you must be at least 18 years old and registered as a participant in the AMD Developer Hackathon: ACT III on lablab.ai.
Each person can redeem the event code only once, so each person can create one event workspace. A team of up to 6 people can work in the same event workspace: one member creates it and invites the others.
Whoever redeems the event code accepts these conditions by ticking the box when redeeming it. Each person invited to an event workspace accepts them by ticking the box the first time they enter the workspace. Nobody can use an event workspace without having accepted these conditions.
We may ask you to confirm that you meet these requirements, and we may close event workspaces used by people who do not.
3
.
How long the event workspace lasts
Your event workspace stays active until 15 November 2026 at 23:59 Italian time. After that, it moves to the Evolus Free plan: its activities stop (for example agents, automations and API calls), but your data stays. 7 days later the workspace is frozen: what goes beyond the Free plan is suspended, not deleted.
At any time you can choose to move the workspace to the paid Developer plan, where it is available to you. That plan is governed by the Evolus General Conditions of Use published at
https://evolus.ai/en/terms-of-use
and by the price list, which you accept separately at that moment; from then on these conditions no longer apply to that workspace.
4
.
Platform credits
Each event workspace receives 50,000 platform credits. They are used by the services that run on the platform's own models, such as document reading, AI Tools and the indexing of knowledge libraries. Calls to the model you connect yourself (section 5) do not use credits.
Credits have no monetary value: they cannot be converted into money, refunded, sold or transferred, and they can be used only in the event workspace that received them, until 15 November 2026 at 23:59 Italian time (section 3). When they run out, the services that need them stop. We may add credits at our discretion, but we are not required to.
5
.
Your own model provider
In event workspaces, agents use a model that you connect yourself, with your own account and key at a provider you choose (for example Fireworks AI, or a server you run on AMD Developer Cloud). You are responsible for your keys, for the costs charged by your provider and for complying with its terms. On your instruction, Evolus sends to the endpoint you configured the content needed to run your agents, such as messages, documents and tool results. Your provider is a third party chosen by you: we are not responsible for its service, availability, costs or handling of data. Protect your endpoint with an API key: without one, anyone who knows its address can use it.
6

Acceptable use
Use the event workspace to build and test your hackathon project. In particular:
use only your own data or test data: do not upload personal data of other people, and never special categories of personal data (for example health data) or data about criminal convictions;
do not attack, probe or overload the platform or other users, and do not try to get around security measures, usage limits or credit counters;
do not resell the workspace or give access to it to people outside your team;
do not create accounts or workspaces automatically or in bulk;
do not use the workspace for unlawful content or activities, or to infringe the rights of others.
Event workspaces have technical limits that keep the platform fair for everyone, including 60 requests per minute per workspace. Requests above a limit are refused.

How we review event workspaces
To check that projects meet the requirements of the Evolus track and to prevent abuse, the Evolus team reviews the configuration and the usage statistics of event workspaces: for example which model is connected, which tools are used and how many API calls are made.

The Evolus platform, its software and its brand remain ours: you get a personal, non-exclusive and non-transferable right to use the event workspace during the event.
If your team wins a prize in the Evolus track, we would like to present your project on evolus.ai. We will do so only with your consent, asked for separately, which you can withdraw at any time.
