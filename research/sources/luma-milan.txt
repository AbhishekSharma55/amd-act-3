About Event
​
Take your AI idea from prototype to working product at the
AMD Developer Hackathon: ACT III in Milan
.
​
This hackathon brings together developers, founders, engineers, students, and AI builders to create high-performance applications and agents using
AMD GPUs in the cloud
.
​
The challenge
​
Build an end-to-end
AI application, agent, or developer tool powered by AMD technology
.
​
Participants will get the opportunity to work with:
​
Cloud-accessible AMD GPUs
​
The ROCm open-source software stack
​
AI and machine learning development resources
​
Technical guidance from mentors and industry experts
​
Training and learning materials from the AMD ecosystem
​
Everything runs in the cloud. You do not need to own AMD hardware or complete a complex local setup before you begin.
​
You can explore AI agents, model training and fine-tuning, workload benchmarking, developer tooling, high-performance machine learning, or an original use case of your own. Detailed tracks will be announced before the kickoff.
​
Build at SmartCityLab Milano
​
The Milan on-site experience will take place at
SmartCityLab Milano
, an innovation hub where technology, sustainability, and design come together to turn ideas into practical projects.
​
Over the weekend, you’ll have space to:
​
Work intensively with your team
​
Exchange ideas with other AI builders
​
Get hands-on technical guidance
​
Test and improve your application
​
Prepare for the final submission
​
Pitch your project live
​
Connect with Milan’s startup and technology community
​
The total hackathon prize pool is
$5,000
.
​
Who is it for?
​
Join us if you are:
​
An AI or machine learning engineer
​
A software developer interested in GPU computing
​
A founder building an AI-powered product
​
A student looking for hands-on technical experience
​
A designer, product builder, or domain expert joining a technical team
​
Curious about AMD GPUs, ROCm, and cloud-based AI development
​
Previous hackathon experience is not required. You can build alone or in a team of up to six people.
