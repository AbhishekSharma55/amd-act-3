About Event
​
Build AI agents and high-performance AI applications on AMD GPUs in the cloud and bring your project to Rome
​
The
AMD Developer Hackathon: ACT III
is an event for developers, founders, engineers, students, and AI builders ready to turn ambitious ideas into working products.
​
On
October 17–18
, participants will meet at
Link Campus University in Rome
to collaborate, receive hands-on support, and showcase their projects live.
​
What you’ll build
​
Create an end-to-end
AI application, agent, or developer tool powered by AMD technology
.
​
You’ll work with
AMD GPUs in the cloud
and the
ROCm open-source software stack
. Everything runs in the cloud, so there is no local hardware to configure — you can focus on experimenting, building, and testing your idea.
​
Possible directions include:
​
AI agents and autonomous workflows
​
High-performance AI and machine learning applications
​
Model training and fine-tuning
​
AI workload benchmarking
​
Developer tools built around AMD’s compute stack
​
Porting and optimizing existing AI workloads for AMD GPUs
​
Tracks and detailed challenges will be announced before the kickoff.
​
Why join us in Rome?
​
The Rome edition will take place at
Link Campus University
, located in the historic Casale di San Pio V campus.
​
During the two-day on-site experience, you’ll be able to:
​
Build alongside other developers and founders
​
Get technical guidance and hands-on support
​
Meet members of the local and international AI community
​
Refine your project before the submission deadline
​
Present your work live during the final pitching session
​
Compete for a share of the
$5,000 prize pool
​
Who should attend?
​
This event is for:
​
AI and machine learning developers
​
Software engineers and technical founders
​
Students and early-career builders
​
Teams exploring AI products and infrastructure
​
Anyone interested in building with AMD GPUs and ROCm
​
All experience levels are welcome. You can participate individually or in a team of up to six people.
