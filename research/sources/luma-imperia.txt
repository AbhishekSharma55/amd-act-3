About Event
​
One hackathon. A global community. Two days of building together on the Ligurian coast.
​
Join the
AMD Developer Hackathon: ACT III in Imperia
and build AI agents, applications, and developer tools using
AMD GPUs in the cloud
and the
ROCm open-source software stack
.
​
What is AMD Developer Hackathon: ACT III?
​
It is a hands-on hackathon for people who want to explore what AI can do on real, high-performance infrastructure.
​
Your goal is to create a working, end-to-end product powered by AMD technology. That could be:
​
An AI agent or multi-agent workflow
​
A high-performance AI application
​
A model training or fine-tuning project
​
A tool for AI developers
​
An experiment benchmarking workloads on AMD GPUs
​
A solution ported or optimized for the ROCm ecosystem
​
Something entirely new
​
All development takes place on cloud-accessible AMD GPUs. There is no need to bring or own specialized hardware.
​
Detailed tracks and challenge requirements will be announced before the kickoff.
​
The Imperia experience
​
The on-site phase will be hosted at
IMPERIAWARE
, Imperia’s digital innovation hub and a space designed to support IT companies, startups, talent, and collaborative technology projects.
​
It is an opportunity to build in a more focused setting while still being part of an international online hackathon.
​
Expect:
​
Two days of hands-on development
​
Collaboration with other builders and teams
​
Mentoring and technical guidance
​
Opportunities to test and improve your project
​
Live project presentations
​
Networking with the technology community
​
A chance to compete for a share of the
$5,000 prize pool
​
Who can participate?
​
The hackathon is open to:
​
Developers and AI engineers
​
Technical and non-technical founders
​
Students and aspiring builders
​
Product designers and domain experts joining technical teams
​
Teams experimenting with AI infrastructure
​
Anyone curious about AMD GPUs and ROCm
​
You do not need previous hackathon experience. Participants can build individually or form teams of up to six people.
