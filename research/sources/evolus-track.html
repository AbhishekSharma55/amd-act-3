How to take part

Three steps.

  1. 1

    Sign up

    Register for the hackathon on lablab.ai and for the AMD AI Developer Program, as lablab asks. Teams have 1 to 6 people.

  2. 2

    Join on October 12, 2026

    At the start of the hackathon we publish the event link on this page and on lablab.ai. Create your workspace only from that link: a workspace created earlier is not an event workspace.

  3. 3

    Connect your model and build

    The agents work on your team's model. The steps are in the developer instructions.

Free, no card, for the whole hackathon and beyond.
