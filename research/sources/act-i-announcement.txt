🏆 10,770 builders walked in. These teams walked out as champions.
The
AMD
Developer Hackathon is officially a wrap!

10K+ participants. 2,621 teams. Nearly 500 submissions. 
What an incredible  turnout. We hope you experienced the power and ease of use of AMD’s MI300X GPUs and the ROCm software stack - with day-0 support for your favorite models.

Your creativity and hard work truly shined. Our judges quickly realized that they had a tough job ahead, each submission required deep evaluation:  demo videos, presentations, GitHub repos, Hugging Face deployments, and even additional research. The competition was extremely close! 

We fully expect several of these projects to become successful startups. 
The global builder community is inspiring - you show up, innovate, and deliver. From advancing drug discovery and building digital medical assistants, to enabling emotional companionship, language learning, fraud detection, historical text preservation, AI-assisted evaluation, and open-source coding tools, you are shaping what’s next. 

🏆Here are your winners

(two first prizes, one third prize)
