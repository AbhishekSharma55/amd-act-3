🏆 Three tracks. Real AMD hardware. 20,000+ builders. And these are the ones who took the podium.

The
AMD
Developer Hackathon: ACT II is a wrap - every winning project built, containerized, and running on AMD Developer Cloud, ROCm, and Fireworks AI. Huge congratulations to the teams that cleared the bar:
🥇 Track 1 - Hybrid Token-Efficient Routing Agent
 1st: LeAgent (leMinou) · 2nd: TERA-v2 (Brute Force) · 3rd: THRIFT (Vyala_dev01)
🎬 Track 2 - Video Captioning
 1st: AI Multilingual Learning Partner (PADAYON) · 2nd: SwiftCap (Technnovative) · 3rd: VeloCap (Allnew)
🦄 Track 3 - Unicorn Track
 1st: Intelligence for Existing CCTV Systems (ROC-Dragon) · 2nd: AgeBand (Kiano) · 3rd: CacheForge (roc-m-roll)

And a shout-out to our top referrers, who helped bring this community together:
 🥇 Muhammad Muteeb Ramzan · 🥈 Jaya Vamsi Krishna Veda · 🥉 Maheswar Sahoo

From token-efficient routing to real-time video captioning to turning existing CCTV into something intelligent - every one of these solved a real problem under real pressure. That's what building on AMD looks like.

👀 One more thing: the winners from our partner
Google DeepMind
are still to come - stay tuned for that announcement!
