Build AI agents and high-performance AI applications on AMD GPUs — entirely in the cloud.

The AMD Developer Hackathon: ACT III is a hands-on event for developers, founders, engineers and builders who want to push what’s possible with AI on real infrastructure. Whether you are just starting out or already shipping, you can jump in and start creating.

You’ll be working with AMD GPUs in the Cloud with the ROCm software stack, AMD's open-source GPU computing platform. Everything runs fully in the cloud, so there is no local hardware to set up and you can focus on building instead of configuration.

One hackathon. Three cities. One special weekend in Italy.

This edition of the AMD Developer Hackathon comes with a special on-site experience across three locations in Italy: Rome, Milan, and Imperia.

The hackathon remains open online throughout the event, while selected participants can also join us in person for the on-site phase. If approved, you’ll choose one of the three locations to build alongside other developers, get hands-on support, and showcase your project live.
