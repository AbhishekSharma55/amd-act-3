Vibe Generation
Vibe Generation is the project founded by Paolo Bonetti to integrate specialized professionals and artificial intelligence tools into the real-world processes of businesses.
The model was created to support companies, brands and organizations in moving from the occasional use of AI tools to building hybrid teams and processes, in which technology and human skills work together.
The project focuses in particular on the relationship between artificial intelligence, talent, judgment and organizational transformation.


Evolus AI
Evolus AI
The platform to build AI agents that run real business processes. Bring your own AMD-hosted model and ship an agent that does real work.

Evolus gives companies AI agents that read documents, answer from company knowledge, run automations and act on the systems they already use through APIs and MCP. Businesses also use Evolus for digital employees that work in email and Microsoft Teams, and for an AI Switchboard that answers the phone.

For developers, everything is driven by the Evolus REST API, agents can also be reached through an OpenAI-compatible endpoint, and an MCP server lets coding agents build on Evolus directly. During the hackathon every team brings its own model.
